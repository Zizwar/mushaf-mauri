# Store Description — English

## App Name
Mushaf Mauri — Holy Quran

## Short Description
Complete Quran app: recitation, memorization, recording, tafsir — Hafs & Warsh

## Full Description

Mushaf Mauri is a comprehensive Quran application that brings together recitation, memorization, recording, and tafsir tools in one place.

Image & Text Mushaf
• High-quality Quran pages in both Hafs (604 pages) and Warsh (638 pages) readings
• Text view with multiple fonts and customizable size and spacing
• Ayah selection and highlighting for follow-along during recitation

Recitation & Memorization
• Over 30 reciters for Hafs reading
• Warsh reciters from the Mohammed VI Foundation for Publishing the Holy Quran
• Ayah and range repetition with full control over repeat count

Voice Recording
• Record your recitation ayah by ayah and compare with the reciter
• Multiple recording profiles to organize your sessions
• Export recordings to share with your teacher

Tafsir & Translation
• Tafsir As-Sa'di, Al-Baghawi, Ibn Katheer, Al-Qurtubi, At-Tabari
• Translations in multiple languages, downloadable for offline reading

Additional Features
• Quran completion (Khatma) tracker with daily progress
• Tasbih & Adhkar counter with smart auto-advance
• Prayer reading mode with auto-scroll and screen lock
• Full-text search across all verses
• Bookmarks and notes
• Offline content download (pages, audio, tafsir databases)
• Multiple themes, colors, and Quran fonts
• 9 supported languages

Completely free — no ads — no restrictions

Content Sources:
• King Saud University (Hafs)
• Mohammed VI Foundation for Publishing the Holy Quran (Warsh)
